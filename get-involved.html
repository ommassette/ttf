Give, partner, or volunteer every contribution reaches a household. We work openly with institutions, networks, and individuals who share a commitment to dignity for all.

Three ways to move with us.

Each path is designed for a different kind of commitment but they all reach the same households, the same wards, the same programmes.

Donate

Financial contributions toward housing restoration, rehabilitation support, bursaries, green-energy access, and economic empowerment for women, widows, and youth.

Who we partner with.

We are open to collaboration with any organisation or institution whose values align with ours and whose work reaches the same households we serve.

Faith communities

Churches and faith networks supporting outreach, rehabilitation, and pastoral care.

Women's & youth networks

Grassroots groups and cooperatives already organised around shared goals.

Legal-aid organisations

Paralegals and advocates supporting widows' succession, land, and property rights.

Clean-energy enterprises

Cookstove manufacturers, solar distributors, and biogas providers.

SACCOs & microfinance

Financial institutions enabling savings, credit, and group enterprise.

Foundations & corporates

Philanthropic and corporate partners funding multi-year programmes.

Government bodies

National and county services delivering public programmes alongside us.

Development partners

Bilateral and multilateral agencies supporting long-term institutional capacity.

Where we work and where we're going.

Programme delivery starts where the Foundation's work is already rooted, then expands region by region as capacity grows.

Phase 1 · 2026–2028

Kenya's North Rift & Western highlands

Programme delivery launches across six counties, building on existing community relationships and ward-level structures.

Nandi Trans Nzoia Baringo Elgeyo Marakwet Bomet Uasin Gishu
Phase 2 · 2029 onwards

South Rift & Coastal communities

Expansion into new regions with the same four-pillar model, adapted to local economies, climate realities, and community structures.

South Rift Coastal Kenya Additional regions as capacity allows
