Institutional governance.

How the Foundation is registered, regulated, and held accountable.

Public Benefit Organisation Status

The Tecla Tum Foundation is registered under the Public Benefit Organisations Act and operates as an independent, non-partisan entity. Registration details are available on request.

Safeguarding

A strict safeguarding policy governs all staff, volunteers, and partners. Zero tolerance for exploitation of any kind.

Financial Transparency

Annual external audits. Public reporting on all programme spending and funding sources.

Independent Board

A Board of Trustees exercising independent oversight of strategy, finance, and programme delivery.
