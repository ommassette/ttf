Strategic Programmes

Four pillars.
One household at a time.

Because vulnerability rarely arrives alone our programmes are designed to work together, meeting a family at every point where it is stretched.

01

Restoring Young Lives

Drug Addiction Prevention, Rehabilitation & Recovery

  • Community outreach, screening, and psychosocial support via churches, schools, and peer networks.
  • Support to residential rehabilitation modelled on the Kaprisang facility.
  • Post-treatment reintegration: vocational training, apprenticeships, mentorship, and start-up kits.
  • Youth sports tournaments for energy redirection.
  • Community mobilisation against illicit brews and drug peddling.
02

Women in Agriculture

Growing Women's Economic Power

  • Farmer field schools focused on climate-smart tea, dairy, poultry, horticulture, and indigenous crops.
  • Access to quality inputs, extension services, and collective marketing via women's cooperatives.
  • Financial literacy training and linkage to affordable credit.
  • Value addition and agro-processing group enterprises.
  • Youth agribusiness incubation.
03

Widows Empowerment

Dignity, Rights, and Livelihoods

  • Legal aid on succession, land, and property rights with paralegals, local chiefs, and courts.
  • Restoration of dilapidated housing structures.
  • Economic enterprise training.
  • Educational support for children in widow-headed households.
04

Women & Climate Resilience

Green Energy for Households and Enterprise

  • Distribution of clean cookstoves ('meko'), biogas, and solar home systems with affordable financing.
  • Technical training for women and youth as solar technicians, cookstove artisans, and green-energy agents.
  • Women- and youth-led tree nurseries and agroforestry projects.
  • Tailored climate information and early-warning alerts for smallholders.
  • Advocacy for women's voices in local climate policy.

Cross-Cutting Themes

Threaded through every programme.

These are not separate projects they are commitments that run across all four pillars, every day, in every ward.

Protection from GBV

Gender-based violence prevention and response embedded in all community work.

Disability Inclusion

Persons with disabilities placed at the centre of programme design and delivery.

Digital Skills

Basic digital literacy and access as a pathway to opportunity for women and youth.

Youth Leadership

Building the next generation of community champions and programme leads.

Strategic Targets

2026–2029. Measurable. Public.

Five commitments we will report against openly.

1

Complete the full recovery-to-reintegration pathway for youth in addiction.

2

Reach 10,000 women farmers with skills, inputs, finance, and markets.

3

Restore homes for 2,000 widows, 500 PWDs, and 1,000 total orphans, and provide school uniforms.

4

Transition 5,000 women-headed households to clean energy and train 1,000 green entrepreneurs.

5

Establish a long-term, well-governed institution built to outlast any single term.
