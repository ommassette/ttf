Eight areas of impact.

Each entry below represents a measurable outcome from the Foundation's founding body of work carried out with partners, community groups, and public institutions.

Addiction & Youth

Sponsored and constructed the Kaprisang Rehabilitation Centre a residential facility providing treatment and recovery support for young people.

Education

Provided bursaries to 3,800 needy students, and sourced funding for 30 Master's students 15+ of whom have graduated.

Asset & Income Support

Distributed 362+ tents and income-generating assets to women, youth, PWD, and church groups. Provided dairy cows to persons with disabilities.

Housing & Dignity

Converted 162 grass-thatched houses into semi-permanent homes restoring shelter and dignity for widow-headed and vulnerable households.

Agricultural Growth

Distributed 5 million avocado seedlings to women and youth groups, and issued economic empowerment grants to 380 groups.

Climate & Health

Distributed 200 'meko' cookstoves to reduce firewood dependence cutting household smoke exposure and easing pressure on local forests.

Policy & Legislation

Championed parliamentary motions on the HPV vaccine for cervical cancer prevention, and contributed to policies on gender, youth, and community safety.

Child Welfare

Spearheaded the Eldoret Municipality street children drop-in centre programme a model built on doctoral research and later continued in partnership with UNICEF.

A record is not a promise.

It is evidence of what becomes possible when resources reach the right hands and a signal of what the Foundation intends to build next. Every figure above began as a household: a child back in school, a roof that holds, a widow with a title deed, a young person in recovery.

The next phase turns that record into an institution with the governance, transparency, and staying power to keep delivering long after any single term.
